Contact FAQ

Quick answers.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Your CNIC or any government ID, previous medical reports or prescriptions, and a list of current medications.

Do you provide emergency care?

Our emergency line is answered 24/7. For life-threatening emergencies, call 1121 or visit the nearest emergency department.

Can I book for a family member?

Yes — book on their behalf and mention their name, age and the reason for the visit in the message field.

Is parking available?

Yes, free visitor parking is available for patients in the plaza basement, with elevator access to the clinic.
