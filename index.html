Questions

Before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form on this page or on the Contact page, or message us on WhatsApp — we'll confirm a slot within the hour.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Yes. Booking in advance is recommended to secure your preferred doctor and reduce waiting time.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes — secure video and phone consultations for many services, from anywhere in Pakistan.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Easily. Message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your slot and we'll rebook it for you.
